Dear Mansi ❤️

♡

Pta nhi is letter ki shuruaat kaha se kru, ya tumse woh sab kaise kahu jo kaafi time se mere dil mein hai. Kuch baatein aisi hoti hain jo hum chaahkar bhi saamne se keh nahi paate, aur shayad mere sath bhi kuch aisa he hai. Isliye aaj socha ki apne dil ki baatein tumhe likhkar btau.

Ye letter likhte waqt thoda ajeeb lag raha hai, lekin saath he achha bhi, kyunki aaj main tumse kuch aisi baatein kehne ja raha hoon jo shayad pehle kabhi khulkar nhi keh paaya.

Pta hai, jab main aaj peeche mudkar dekhta hu, to kabhi-kabhi khud bhi sochta hu ki ye sb shuru kaise hua. Jaane kb tum mujhe itni achhi lagne lagi. Shuruaat mein bs ek chhoti si liking thi, lekin dheere-dheere tumhare baare mein aur jaanne ka man hone lga. Shayad us waqt mujhe khud bhi nhi pta tha ki ye chhoti si liking kab itni gehri feeling ban jaayegi. Phir jab humari baatein shuru hui aur dheere-dheere hum friends se best friends bane, to tum meri life ka ek aisa hissa ban gayi jiske saath baat karna, apni baatein share karna aur din ke chhote-chhote moments btana achha lagne lga. Aur shayad isi safar mein, bina mujhe khud pta chle, tum mere liye friendship se kahi zyada special ban gayi.

Pta nahi tumhari aisi kaunsi-kaunsi baatein hain jo mujhe itni achhi lagti hain ki kabhi-kabhi khud bhi explain nhi kr pata. Tumse baat karna, tumhari baaton ko sunna, tumhari smile aur tumhara woh cute sa nature, ye sb mujhe bahut achha lagta hai. Aur sirf tumhari achhi baatein hi nahi, tum jaisi ho, waise he mujhe achhi lagti ho. Tumhara apna tareeka aur tumhari chhoti-badi baatein mujhe tumhari taraf aur attract karti hain. Shayad isi wajah se tum mere liye itni important ho, aur tumhare liye jo feel karta hoon, woh sirf ek normal si liking nahi hai.

Aur sach kahu to ab mujhe lgta hai ki jo feeling mere man mein tumhare liye hai, unhe sirf friendship kehna shayad sahi nahi hoga. I think, I have started liking you in a way that's a little more than just a friendship. Aur shayad ab mujhe ehsaas ho rha hai ki main sirf tumhe pasand he nhi krta, balki tumse pyaar bhi karne laga hoon. And honestly, Mansi, I think I've fallen in love with you. Ye baat tumse kehne ka man toh kaafi baar hua, par har baar samajh nhi aaya ki kaise kahu. Pta nhi tum ise kaise logi, ya ye sab kehna sahi rahega bhi ya nhi. Pr aaj laga ki apne man ki baat tumse keh deni chahiye, kyuki kuch feelings aisi hoti hai jinhe zyada der apne andar rakhna bhi mushkil ho jata hai.

Jo bhi tum feel krti ho, main chahta hoon ki tum bina kisi hesitation ke apni baat keh sko.

Bas aakhir mein itna he kehna hai ki tum mere liye sach mein bahut special ho, aur shayad main ye baat words mein kabhi poori tarah explain nhi kr paunga. Main bs chahta hoon ki tum hamesha khush rho, tumhari smile hamesha aise he bani rahe aur life mein tumhe woh sab mile jo tum chahti ho. Tumse ye sab kehna mere liye aasaan nhi tha, pr achha lag raha hai ki aaj kam se kam apne dil ki baat tum tak pahunchaa paaya.
